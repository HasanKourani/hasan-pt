Fair questions

What's your coaching background?

Three years of serious lifting, plus one-on-one coaching where everyone I've trained has seen results. Coaching is now my business, and I'm keeping the roster small so every client gets real attention. The first chat is free, so you can see if we're a fit, and there's no contract.

I've never trained before. Is that OK?

That's the best place to start. We begin with the basics, learn the movements properly and build from there. No one is judging where you start.

Online or in person: which should I pick?

If you already know your way around a gym and want structure and accountability, online is enough. If you're new, or want someone checking your form every set, go 1-on-1. Still unsure? Ask in the free chat.

How do the weekly check-ins work?

Once a week you send me your weight, how your workouts went and, if you want, a photo. I look at the numbers and adjust your plan for the next week. It takes you about five minutes.

Where do the 1-on-1 sessions happen?

At the gym, at times that suit us both. We agree on the place and schedule on WhatsApp before you commit to anything.

How do I pay, and can I stop?

We sort out payment on WhatsApp. Coaching is month to month with no contract, so you can stop whenever you want.
